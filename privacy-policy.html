Privacy Policy for Formula Calc

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Formula Calc is a calculator application designed to perform mathematical and formula-based calculations.

Information We Collect

Formula Calc does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Calculations and information entered into the app are processed locally on your device. Formula Calc does not transmit this information to us or to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Formula Calc does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or data-collection services.

Children's Privacy

Formula Calc does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be published at this URL with a new revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing for Formula Calc.
